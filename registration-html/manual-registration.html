QUICK GUIDE

Manual alignment

  1. Select mRNA or TAPS as the active layer.
  2. The inactive moving modality is hidden automatically.
  3. Use the row/column controls, arrow keys, or drag the overlay to translate the moving image.
  4. Drag the overlay for free translation. Hold Space while dragging to pan the view.
  5. Adjust rotation and opacity as needed.
  6. Export row/column hires-pixel offsets and rotation as JSON.
